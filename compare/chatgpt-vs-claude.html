Officially both cost $20/month on web (~Rs. 5,700–6,900+ via SadaPay or bank cards with taxes) and Rs. 4,900/month on the Pakistan Apple App Store. On App Store yearly plans, ChatGPT Plus annual is ~Rs. 49,900/year vs Claude Pro ~Rs. 59,900/year. Should Pakistani software developers pick ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro?

Most full-stack developers prefer Claude Pro for multi-file codebase context (200K+ tokens), Artifacts UI previews, and cleaner long-form code refactoring. Choose ChatGPT Plus if you need Voice Mode, native image generation, Deep Research web workflows, or OpenAI's lower annual App Store tier.
