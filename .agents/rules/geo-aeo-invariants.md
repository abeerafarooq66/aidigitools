# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Invariants

## 1. Core Directives
1. **Direct Answer Extraction (AEO / AIO Primary Snippet)**:
   - Provide a concise 35–70 word direct answer or definition immediately under the primary `<h1>`.
   - Optimal target: **40–60 words**.
   - Must be factual, self-contained, and devoid of generic throat-clearing fluff ("In this modern era...", "As we know...").
2. **5-Branch Query Fan-Out**:
   - Every product and guide page must explicitly answer:
     * **Pricing**: Explicit numerical PKR price and USD equivalent.
     * **Payment Rails**: Domestic (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay, Raast, Bank IBFT) and Global (Wise, Crypto USDT).
     * **Delivery SLA**: 15–30 minute digital delivery via WhatsApp helpline `03326861112`.
     * **Warranty & Replacement**: 30-day replacement warranty.
     * **Audience & Use-Case**: Freelancers, students, content creators, developers, and digital marketing agencies.
3. **Structured HTML Elements**:
   - Use semantic `<table>` elements with `<thead>` and `<tbody>` for side-by-side tool and plan comparisons.
   - Use `<ol>` for sequential activation steps.
   - Use `<details>` and `<summary>` for visible FAQ accordions with corresponding `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.
4. **GEO Content Sequence**:
   - Section 1: Direct Answer (`.quick-answer`)
   - Section 2: Why It Matters (Context & Problem Breakdown)
   - Section 3: Technical Specifications & Features
   - Section 4: 4-Row Quantitative Comparison Table
   - Section 5: First-Hand Testing Insight
   - Section 6: Visible Interactive FAQ Accordion
   - Section 7: Source Attributions, Freshness Date & Reseller Notice
5. **No Synthetic AI Rewriting**:
   - Never rewrite content into unnatural robot prose. Write for real human readers using natural conversational phrasing that generative search engines can cite with high confidence.
