# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Invariants

## Core Directives
1. **Direct Answer Extraction**:
   - Provide a concise 35–70 word definition or answer immediately under the primary `<h1>` or `<h2>`.
   - Avoid generic introductory fluff ("In today's fast-paced digital world...").
2. **5-Branch Query Fan-Out**:
   - Ensure explicit answers for:
     * Pricing in PKR
     * Payment methods (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, Raast)
     * Delivery speed (Instant / within 15–30 minutes)
     * Replacement warranty and validity duration
     * Use case compatibility (freelancers, content creators, students)
3. **Structured HTML Elements**:
   - Use semantic `<table>` elements with `<thead>` and `<tbody>` for side-by-side tool and plan comparisons.
   - Use `<ol>` for sequential activation steps.
   - Use `<dl>` or structured FAQ accordions with corresponding `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.
4. **Machine Knowledge Indexing**:
   - Maintain parity between live catalog pages, `/llms.txt`, and `/llms-full.txt`.
