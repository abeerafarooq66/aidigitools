# Keyword Strategy & Content Creation System

## 1. The 14 Keyword Types
1. **Short-tail**: High search volume, broad category level (`AI tools`). Target: Homepage & Hub pages.
2. **Long-tail**: High intent, lower volume, high conversion (`best AI video generator for beginners in Pakistan`). Target: Guides and Comparisons.
3. **Branded**: Direct entity awareness (`AIDigiTools AI marketplace`). Target: Homepage, About, Contact.
4. **Non-branded**: Problem-oriented solution discovery (`Canva templates for Instagram`). Target: Product and Category pages.
5. **Informational**: Educational queries (`What is AI video editing?`). Target: Guides, Blogs, FAQs.
6. **Commercial**: Evaluative queries (`Best AI tools for creators`). Target: Best-of listicles and Compare hubs.
7. **Transactional**: Purchase-intent queries (`Buy Canva Pro subscription with EasyPaisa`). Target: Product pages and Pricing tables.
8. **Navigational**: Exact website lookup (`AIDigiTools official site`). Target: Meta titles, footer anchors.
9. **Local & Regional**: Geographic queries (`AI software subscriptions Islamabad Pakistan`). Target: Localized content, Policies, Contact.
10. **Question-based**: Problem solvers (`How to create AI video from text?`). Target: Quick Answer blocks, FAQ sections.
11. **Comparison**: Versus evaluation (`ChatGPT vs Claude`). Target: Dedicated Comparison pages.
12. **Alternative**: Substitute finders (`Canva alternatives`). Target: Alternative articles and tool grids.
13. **Pricing**: Cost evaluation (`AI video tool pricing in PKR`). Target: Pricing tables and Product specs.
14. **Problem-based**: Pain-point queries (`fix credit card declined on international AI tools`). Target: Solutions and FAQ sections.

- **Unique Angle / Information Gain**: (First-hand benchmark or localized PKR pricing table)
- **Original Assets Needed**: (Branded 1200x630 banner and UI preview card)
- **FAQs to Include**: (4–8 real People Also Ask questions)
- **Structured Data / Schema**: (`Product` + `Offer` OR `Article` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`)
- **Success Metrics**: (Indexed status, CTR in GSC, Conversions via WhatsApp)

## 3. Article Structure Standard
- `H1`: Primary Keyword + User Benefit
- `Intro`: Problem definition + Solution promise + `.quick-answer` (40–60 words)
- `H2`: What is [Entity/Tool]? (Core definition & primary capabilities)
- `H2`: Why It Matters (Key bottlenecks solved, workflow enhancements)
- `H2`: Pricing & Plan Comparison (Structured semantic HTML `<table>` with PKR & USD figures)
- `H2`: First-Hand Testing Observations (Real speed, token limits, and rendering observations)
- `H2`: Step-by-Step Activation Guide (`<ol>` with 3–5 simple ordering steps)
- `H2`: Pros and Cons (Honest assessment of strengths and limitations)
- `H2`: Frequently Asked Questions (`<details>` accordions with visible answers)
- `CTA`: Official Order Concierge (`https://wa.me/923326861112`)
