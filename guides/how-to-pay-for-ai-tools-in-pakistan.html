Why Pakistani Bank Cards Fail on OpenAI, Claude & Midjourney (And Real Cost With Taxes)

Global AI platforms such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, and Midjourney process recurring billing through US-dollar payment gateways like Stripe and Paddle. Neither OpenAI nor Anthropic accepts EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or Raast directly on web checkout, and standard Pakistani commercial debit cards frequently decline due to bank e-commerce blocks or 3D Secure risk checks.

When paying a $20 USD/month AI plan directly with a Pakistani card, buyers also incur currency conversion spreads (roughly 3%–3.5% on typical cards) plus State Bank international withholding taxes (5% for active FBR tax filers and 10% for non-filers), bringing effective monthly billing to approximately Rs. 5,700–7,000 PKR.

Method 1 (Self-Serve): Step-by-Step Payment via SadaPay, NayaPay & Raast

If you want direct billing in your own name on OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, or Midjourney without a traditional bank credit card, Pakistani Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) like SadaPay (Mastercard) and NayaPay (Visa) allow virtual debit card payments funded from EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or any bank via Raast.

  1. Open & Verify Wallet: Download SadaPay or NayaPay from Google Play or the Apple App Store and complete biometric/CNIC verification.
  2. Transfer PKR via Raast or IBFT: Open EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or your commercial bank app (Meezan, HBL, UBL, Alfalah) and send PKR using Raast ID (your phone number) or your wallet IBAN.
  3. Buffer 12%–15% Extra Balance: For a $20 USD subscription (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, or Cursor Pro), load at least Rs. 6,200 to Rs. 7,000 PKR so SBP withholding tax (5% filer / 10% non-filer) and FX spreads do not cause an insufficient-balance decline.
  4. Enable International E-Commerce: Inside your SadaPay or NayaPay app card controls, toggle International / Online Transactions ON before visiting checkout.
  5. Complete Checkout & 3DS OTP: Enter your Virtual Card Number, Expiry Date, and CVV on chatgpt.com, claude.ai, or midjourney.com and approve any 3D Secure prompt.

Decline Troubleshooting Tip: If Stripe declines your virtual card twice in a row, stop retrying immediately—repeated failed authorizations can trigger temporary card locks on SadaPay or NayaPay. Verify your PKR buffer first or switch to Method 2 or Method 3 below.
