Can I buy Claude Pro with EasyPaisa or JazzCash directly?

Anthropic does not accept EasyPaisa or JazzCash wallets directly on claude.ai. Pakistani buyers either fund a SadaPay or NayaPay virtual card via Raast to pay Anthropic directly, or settle fixed PKR via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, or Raast through AIDigiTools on WhatsApp (+92 332 6861112).

Why do Pakistani debit cards get declined on Anthropic Claude Pro?

Anthropic Stripe billing checks require international e-commerce permissions, active 3D Secure OTP, exact billing-address matching, and SMS-verified account phone numbers. Enabling foreign transactions on SadaPay or NayaPay with a Rs. 6,500+ buffer fixes most direct web declines, or users switch to Apple App Store PKR billing or local PKR concierge settlement.
